# VOLCANE – Consumer Demand & Market Feasibility Study

## Project Context

VOLCANE is a fermented beverage concept (sugarcane-and-fruit enzyme drink) developed as
part of a 6-member group project for the *Investment Project Appraisal* course at the
University of Economics and Law, VNU-HCM. The full group deliverable was a comprehensive
investment feasibility study covering market research, technical/operations planning,
environmental compliance, and financial analysis.

**This repository covers only the consumer and market research component that I
personally led.** Other parts of the group's full feasibility study — production
technology, facility planning, cost of capital, and legal/environmental analysis — were
handled by other team members and are outside the scope of this repository.

## My Role: Market & Pricing Research

I was responsible for:
- Designing the consumer survey instrument
- Collecting primary data from the target consumer segment
- Analyzing consumer behavior and demand patterns
- Researching the niche market opportunity
- Researching and testing selling price points

## Objective

To assess whether real demand existed for a healthier, low-sugar fermented beverage among
students and young consumers in the university-village area, and to identify the price
point and consumer segment most likely to support the product's launch.

## Methodology

**Sample size:** Determined using Slovin's formula, cross-checked against the minimum
sample-size guidance for exploratory factor analysis (Hair et al., 2014). The target of
~100 respondents was exceeded, with **127 valid responses** collected.

**Sampling method:** Non-probability convenience sampling, distributed through online
channels (Facebook and Zalo student groups) and in person around student dormitories, with
responses spread across multiple sub-areas to maintain reasonable representativeness.

**Data collection tool:** Structured questionnaire administered via Google Forms, covering
respondent demographics, beverage consumption habits, purchase channels, product concept
reception, willingness to pay, and price sensitivity.

**Analysis approach:** Descriptive and behavioral analysis of survey responses, segmentation
by demographics and spending capacity, and a price-sensitivity comparison across two price
scenarios (30,000 VND vs. 35,000 VND per serving).

## Key Findings

- **Demand gap:** A majority of respondents cited safety/hygiene concerns and excessive
  sugar content as pain points with existing beverage options, pointing to unmet demand for
  a healthier, natural-sweetness alternative.
- **Purchase intent:** The large majority of respondents indicated a high level of
  willingness to try and buy the product concept.
- **Price sensitivity:** 30,000 VND per serving was identified as the price point that
  maximized purchase intent; raising the price to 35,000 VND caused a sharp drop-off in
  willingness to buy, marking it as a resistance threshold.
- **Target niche:** Health-conscious, budget-constrained young consumers living or studying
  in the university-village area emerged as the clearest target segment, shaping
  recommendations on pricing and distribution channels (fixed kiosks and mobile carts).
