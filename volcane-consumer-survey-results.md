# VOLCANE Consumer Survey Results
### Demand Survey for Sugarcane & Fruit Enzyme Water — VOLCANE

> This survey was conducted by a student team developing **VOLCANE**, an enzyme-fermented sugarcane and fruit beverage project targeting the Vietnam National University HCMC "University Village" (Làng Đại học) area. The goal was to understand student demand, consumption behavior, and interest in healthy beverage products, to inform market analysis, business strategy, and project feasibility.

**Total responses:** 127

## Part II — Consumption Habits & Current Market

### Q5. On average per month, how often do you consume beverages away from home (milk tea, sugarcane juice, fresh juice, coffee, etc.)?

| Answer | % |
|---|---|
| Less than 4 times/month (under 1x/week) | 17.3% |
| 4–8 times/month (~1–2x/week) | 34.6% |
| 9–15 times/month (~3–4x/week) | 25.2% |
| Over 15 times/month (almost daily) | 22.8% |

### Q6. What time of day do you most often buy/drink beverages?
*(Multiple choice)*

| Answer | Responses | % |
|---|---|---|
| Morning (7:00–10:00) | 18 | 14.2% |
| Midday/early afternoon (10:00–16:00) | 67 | 52.8% |
| Evening (after 16:00–21:00) | 42 | 33.1% |
| No fixed time, whenever I feel like it | 49 | 38.6% |

### Q7. Which type of beverage do you choose most often in the University Village? *(Choose up to 3)*

| Answer | Responses | % |
|---|---|---|
| Traditional sugarcane juice / street-side fresh fruit juice | 79 | 62.2% |
| Milk tea / sugary, high-fat fruit tea | 82 | 64.6% |
| Bottled beverages (kombucha, green tea, soda, etc.) | 50 | 39.4% |
| Coffee / "bạc xỉu" (Vietnamese milk coffee) | 54 | 42.5% |
| Other healthy drinks (detox water, drinking yogurt, etc.) | 37 | 29.1% |

### Q8. Which sales channel do you most often use to buy beverages daily in the University Village? *(Choose up to 2)*

| Answer | Responses | % |
|---|---|---|
| School / dormitory canteen | 60 | 47.2% |
| Street carts / stalls along University Village roads | 75 | 59.1% |
| Fixed café/milk tea chains with seating (Zone B services area, Highlands, etc.) | 84 | 66.1% |
| Convenience stores / mini-marts (GS25, FamilyMart, Circle K, etc.) | 56 | 44.1% |
| Food delivery apps (GrabFood, ShopeeFood, etc.) | 55 | 43.3% |

### Q9. Average total amount spent per WEEK on beverages purchased outside

| Answer | % |
|---|---|
| Under 50,000 VND | 14.2% |
| 50,000 – under 100,000 VND | 8.7% |
| 100,000 – under 200,000 VND | 33.9% |
| 200,000 – under 300,000 VND | 33.1% |
| Over 300,000 VND | — |

### Q10. When using current beverages in the University Village (street sugarcane juice, cart milk tea, etc.), what concerns/dissatisfies you the most? *(Multiple choice)*

| Answer | Responses | % |
|---|---|---|
| Food safety/hygiene issues (dirty ice, flies/insects, reused cups, roadside dust) | 106 | 83.5% |
| Too much sugar (risk of weight gain, unhealthy) | 92 | 72.4% |
| Heavy use of artificial flavoring, coloring, or preservatives | 78 | 61.4% |
| Unclear ingredient origin, no specific nutritional benefit | 91 | 71.7% |
| Price too high for a student budget | 50 | 39.4% |
| Generic flavor, lacking innovation or uniqueness | 44 | 34.6% |

## Part III — Purchase Criteria & Health Trend Awareness

### Q11. For premium bottled healthy products (kombucha, macrobiotic tea, etc.) already on the market, what is the biggest reason you DON'T use them regularly?

| Answer | Responses | % |
|---|---|---|
| Price too high for a student budget | 81 | 63.8% |
| Strong sour taste / carbonation, hard to get used to | 57 | 44.9% |
| Hard to find for direct purchase near the University Village | 64 | 50.4% |
| Brand not well-known, unclear actual benefits | 67 | 52.8% |
| I already use these regularly | 2 | 1.6% |

### Q12. When choosing a beverage, how IMPORTANT are the following factors to you? *(Scale 1 = not at all important, 5 = extremely important)*

Factors rated:
- Price fits a student budget
- Great taste, easy to drink, suits personal preference
- Food safety and clean processing standards
- Health benefits (low sugar, no saturated fat)
- Convenient location near UEL/dorm area

*(Results shown as a distribution chart across the 1–5 scale in the original survey; most responses skewed toward 4–5, indicating high importance across all factors, especially taste and hygiene/safety.)*

### Q13. To what extent do you AGREE with the following statements about health trends? *(Scale 1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree)*

Statements rated:
- I care about gut and digestive health
- I am actively cutting down on unhealthy drinks high in fat or artificial sugar
- I am willing to pay a reasonable price for organic, natural products

*(Results shown as a distribution chart; most responses skewed toward 4–5, indicating strong agreement.)*

### Q14. What do you think of combining "sugarcane juice" (a familiar, everyday drink) with "natural enzyme fermentation with fruit"?

| Answer | % |
|---|---|
| Sounds very novel and unique, sparks curiosity and high expectations | 78.0% |
| Seems ordinary, nothing special | 9.4% |
| Doesn't quite make sense, hard to imagine the taste | 12.6% |

## Part IV — Reception of the VOLCANE Product Concept

> **Product description shown to respondents:** VOLCANE is a next-generation healthy beverage made from pure fresh sugarcane juice (fully replacing refined sugar) combined with tropical fruit, undergoing a natural enzyme fermentation process. It retains a light, refreshing sweetness, is rich in vitamins, and adds beneficial probiotics for digestion — with NO artificial sugar and NO industrial preservatives.

### Q15. After reading the description above, how APPEALING and how READY are you to try VOLCANE if it appeared in the University Village? *(Scale 1 = not ready at all, 5 = very ready)*

| Answer | % |
|---|---|
| Not appealing / don't want to try it at all | — |
| Not ready to try | 16.5% |
| Neutral / would consider it based on friends' reviews or a promotion | 51.2% |
| Ready to try it | 29.9% |
| Very ready to buy immediately at launch and support long-term | — |

### Q16. What is the biggest differentiator/reason that makes you impressed and interested in trying VOLCANE?

| Answer | % |
|---|---|
| No industrial sugar or fat — natural sweetness from fresh sugarcane juice | 11.8% |
| Rich in fresh enzymes and probiotics, good for gut/digestive health | 15.7% |
| Novelty of the "fermented sugarcane with fruit" concept | 38.6% |
| Guaranteed hygiene standards, clear/clean production process | 33.9% |

### Q17. When trying a completely new fermented product line like VOLCANE, what makes you most hesitant or unsure before deciding to buy?

| Answer | % |
|---|---|
| Worried the fermented taste will be too sour or hard to drink, not suited to student taste | 14.2% |
| Worried the handmade production process won't meet gut-health/food-safety standards | 17.3% |
| Worried the actual price will be higher than street beverages | 33.1% |
| I'm completely excited to try it, no hesitation at all | 35.4% |

### Q18. Among the planned tropical fruit flavors below, which do you most want to try? *(Choose up to 3)*

| Flavor | Responses | % |
|---|---|---|
| VOLCANE Original Sugarcane (pure, rustic fermented sugarcane) | 92 | 72.4% |
| VOLCANE Passion Fruit (bold sweet-and-sour, cooling) | 64 | 50.4% |
| VOLCANE Pineapple (mild sweetness, fragrant) | 60 | 47.2% |
| VOLCANE Green Apple (light, refreshing) | 60 | 47.2% |
| VOLCANE Natural Orange (mildly sweet, rich in vitamin C) | 59 | 46.5% |
| VOLCANE Young Coconut | 1 | 0.8% |

### Q19. Which packaging format for VOLCANE (500ml) would feel the most convenient for student life and most likely to prompt a purchase?

| Answer | % |
|---|---|
| Eco-friendly sealed paper cup, 500ml (ready-to-drink like milk tea) | 7.9% |
| PET plastic bottle with cap, 350–500ml (lightweight, easy to carry in a backpack/on the bus) | 28.3% |
| Glass bottle (attractive design, eco-friendly, reusable) | 63.8% |

## Part V — Willingness to Pay & Marketing Channels

### Q20. For a 500ml healthy beverage like VOLCANE, what price would you find easy to pay and would encourage you to buy regularly?

| Answer | % |
|---|---|
| 20,000 – under 25,000 VND | 14.2% |
| 25,000 – 30,000 VND | 55.9% |
| 31,000 – 35,000 VND | 27.6% |
| Over 35,000 VND | — |

### Q21. How does your willingness to buy change under the following hypothetical pricing scenarios? *(Scale 1 = definitely won't buy, 5 = definitely will buy)*

Scenarios rated:
- Official price set at 30,000 VND/cup
- Price raised to 35,000 VND/cup

*(Results shown as a distribution chart; willingness to buy notably declines at the higher price point.)*

### Q22. Which sales/distribution model would you most want for easy access to VOLCANE in the University Village? *(Multiple choice)*

| Answer | Responses | % |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed kiosk in the central area of Dorm A | 47 | 37.0% |
| Fixed kiosk in the central area of Dorm B | 83 | 65.4% |
| Mobile cart at streets with high student-housing density | 82 | 64.6% |
| Online storefront via Fanpage/Zalo or delivery apps | 72 | 56.7% |
| Free fast in-campus delivery service to encourage repeat purchases | 69 | 54.3% |
